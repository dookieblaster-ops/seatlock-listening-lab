# ASR measured curves for the Criton 3TD-X

CTA-2034 curves of the CSS Criton 3TD-X, measured by Audio Science Review on a Klippel Near-Field Scanner and published through the spinorama project.

## Credit and license

- **Measurement:** Audio Science Review. The spinorama speaker page is https://www.spinorama.org/speakers/CSS%20Criton%203TD-X%20DIY/ASR/index_asr.html . ASR's public measurement list dates the review 2024-11-12.
- **Published data:** https://www.spinorama.org/speakers/CSS%20Criton%203TD-X%20DIY/ASR/asr/CEA2034.json
- **License:** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Credit Audio Science Review and the spinorama project.
- **Source file read:** the plotly `CEA2034.json` on 2026-11-15. On-axis, listening window, early reflections, and sound power were taken from that file (194 points, about 20 Hz to 20 kHz). Levels are relative.

On the spinorama page: measured sensitivity 86.8 dB (300 Hz–3 kHz, 2.83 V, 1 m), company figure 87.8 dB, preference score 4.68 (5.42 with EQ).

Against the lab's `criton-3td-x` everyday model with the kit passive network, after aligning midband means (300 Hz to 5 kHz):

- Midband and treble shapes are within about 2 dB RMS of ASR.
- Bass relative to that midband is about 5 dB higher in the lab than in the ASR file (lab about +5 dB, ASR about −0.5 dB), after the cabinet was set from the drawing and the assembly guide (front top box, woofer air behind it, 3 × 8 in port).
